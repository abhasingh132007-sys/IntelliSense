About the Project

What IntelliSense actually does

IntelliSense is a web-based Intrusion Detection and Prevention System (IDPS) built around a real security team workflow, not just a single admin panel. A SOC Analyst investigates live traffic, assigns severity, and recommends action. An Administrator reviews escalated incidents and approves or rejects the recommended response — the same investigate → escalate → approve pattern used in real security operations centers.

Every incident's notes, recommendation, and final decision are stored, giving your organization a clear audit trail of what happened and who approved it.

Why IntelliSense

Built to protect and to teach
